**Clean ad data in. Trusted reports out.**

GUARD loads daily files from ad networks into PostgreSQL, explains every rejected row, protects personal data,
backs everything up (and proves the backups restore), monitors itself and gives business users a read-only console.

> A portfolio project on synthetic data. Polish README: [GitHub](https://github.com/<your-username>/guard#readme).
